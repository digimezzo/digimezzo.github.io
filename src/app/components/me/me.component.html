Hi!

I'm Raphaël, 47, from Belgium. I'm your friendly neighbourhood software developer and the creator of Dopamine, Knowte and Vitomu. I share my projects under the name DigimezZo, short for "Digital intermezzo": a little break in the digital world.

My passion for software began when I got my first computer as a teenager. That curiosity led me to start creating software of my own, and I've been doing it for more than 20 years now. I work on these projects in my spare time, simply because I enjoy making software.

I believe software should work with you, not against you. My goal is to create applications that are clear, easy to use and stay out of your way, without unnecessary interruptions or nagging, so you can focus on what you want to do and enjoy doing it.

Thanks for stopping by, and I hope you enjoy using my software! ♥️
